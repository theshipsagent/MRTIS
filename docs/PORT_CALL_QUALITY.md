## Geofence artefacts

Only the first docking and the last sailing of a visit are read as operations (William, 2026-08-19). Overlapping geofences and movement within a berth produce the rest: a large ocean vessel does not dock, sail and redock in minutes.

- Berth events collapsed as artefacts: **5,102** of 96,845 (5.3%). They stay on the spine, flagged `is_geofence_artifact`; they are simply not read as arrivals or sailings, and the visit's draft change is measured across them.
- Legs containing at least one: 3,420

## Agency fee

- Legs that never reached a berth and accrue nothing: 1,415
- `No Cargo` legs that berthed but worked no cargo (bunkers, stores, repair, lay-by): 421, currently charged $3,783,500 -- **open**: a ship at a berth is still being agented, but confirm.
- Chargeable legs whose activity is unresolved: 5,722 -- the split rule never lets an unknown invent a split, so genuine Discharge->Load calls in here are still being billed once. The total is a floor.

The per-departure figure is carried through on `port_call_event.agency_fee` unchanged and reconciled by a hard guardrail, so the two bases can be compared row by row. Do not sum that column for billing.

## Time

Waiting time is only anchorage dwell **before** the leg's first berth arrival -- time spent waiting for that berth. Dwell after the last sailing is outbound idling and is reported separately so it can never be counted as berth waiting.
